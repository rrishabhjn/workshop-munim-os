# Staff Privacy Notice / कर्मचारी गोपनीयता सूचना

_Print this, fill in the blanks, read it out at a team meeting, and post it in the Telegram Attendance group. Keep a signed copy, or a 👍 reply in the group, as a record of consent._

---

### Yeh notice kis baare mein hai? (What is this about?)

Workshop ab hisaab-kitaab, production aur attendance ke liye ek computer system (**Workshop Munim**) use karta hai. Is mein aapki kuch jaankari rakhi jaati hai. Yeh notice batata hai kya rakha jaata hai, kyun, aur kab tak.

*The workshop uses a computer system called Workshop Munim for accounts, production and attendance. This notice explains what information about you it keeps, why, and for how long.*

### Kya jaankari li jaati hai? (What we collect)

- 📸 **Attendance photo:** jab aap Telegram Attendance group mein aane aur jaane ki photo bhejte hain, photo aur uska time. *(Check-in/out photos and their time.)*
- ⏱️ **Ghante aur overtime:** roz ke kaam ke ghante, aur shaam 6:00 baje ke baad ka overtime. *(Daily hours and overtime after 6:00 PM.)*
- 💰 **Salary aur advance:** mahine ki salary, advance, aur jis bank account mein payment jaata hai uska naam. *(Salary, advances, and the name on your payout account.)*
- 📱 **Naam aur Telegram ID:** taaki system pehchaan sake photo kisne bheji. *(Your name and Telegram ID, so the system knows who sent a photo.)*

❌ Aadhaar, PAN, face-recognition ya GPS location **nahi** liya jaata. *(No Aadhaar, PAN, face recognition or GPS.)*

### Kyun? (Why)

Sirf aapki **haazri, overtime aur salary ka sahi hisaab** rakhne ke liye. Is jaankari ko bechte nahi hain aur kisi customer ko nahi dikhate.

*Only to calculate your attendance, overtime and pay correctly. It is never sold or shown to customers.*

### Kaun dekh sakta hai? (Who can see it)

Sirf workshop owner aur office staff (accounts/clerk). Data India (Mumbai) ke secure servers par rakha jaata hai. Messages samajhne ke liye Google ki AI service aur Telegram bhi use hote hain.

*Only the owner and office staff. Data is stored on secure servers in Mumbai, India. Google's AI service and Telegram are also used to process messages.*

### Kab tak? (How long)

- Attendance **photo: 90 din** baad delete. *(Deleted after 90 days.)*
- Ghante, salary aur payment ka record: **8 saal** (kanoon ke hisaab se). *(8 years, as required by law.)*

### Aapke adhikaar (Your rights)

Aap kabhi bhi:
- apni jaankari dekhne ya galti theek karwane ke liye keh sakte hain, *(see or correct your data)*
- photo attendance ki jagah **register mein haazri** ka option maang sakte hain, isse aapki naukri ya salary par koi asar nahi padega, *(choose a paper register instead of photos; this will not affect your job or pay)*
- naukri chhodne ke baad apna contact data hatwa sakte hain (salary/hisaab ka record kanoon ke liye rakha jaayega). *(have your contact details removed after leaving; legally required pay records are kept.)*
